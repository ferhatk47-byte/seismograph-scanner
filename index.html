If an exchange shows "blocked"

Browsers only let a page read another site's data when that site allows it (CORS). Binance does. If Bybit, OKX or KuCoin refuse, the scan carries on with the others. To bring a blocked exchange back, deploy this free Cloudflare Worker and paste its URL into Settings. It only forwards to these three exchange hosts.

export default {
  async fetch(req) {
    const u = new URL(req.url).searchParams.get("url") || "";
    if (!/^https:\/\/(api\.bybit\.com|www\.okx\.com|api\.kucoin\.com)\//.test(u))
      return new Response("host not allowed", { status: 400 });
    const r = await fetch(u, { headers: { "User-Agent": "seismograph" } });
    return new Response(r.body, { status: r.status, headers: {
      "Content-Type": "application/json", "Access-Control-Allow-Origin": "*" } });
  }
};

Not available for free

  • Exchange reserves and net flows: CryptoQuant, Glassnode and Arkham sell this. Building it from public explorers would only see known hot wallets, a lower bound that can mislead, so the rule stays empty.
  • Staking ratio outside Cosmos and Solana: StakingRewards has no free API plan.
  • X/Twitter follower counts: CoinGecko stopped returning community data on 28 Aug 2026. News coverage velocity stands in for social attention.
  • DefiLlama unlock data: moved to its paid Pro API. Mobula's free plan covers unlocks instead.

Four exchanges, one universe

USDT spot pairs from Binance, Bybit, OKX and KuCoin are merged by ticker symbol. Volume is summed across venues for the universe filter. Each coin's candles come from its primary venue, the one with the most 24h volume, so its volume history stays on one consistent book. Order-book depth is summed across every venue that lists it.

Stablecoins and tokenized stocks are left out by default, judged from price behaviour rather than names: a coin whose daily moves stay under 0.4% with price near $1 is a stablecoin; one whose weekend moves are under 35% of its weekday moves is tracking a market that closes on weekends. Against BTC, a stablecoin is just BTC upside down, so it distorts the "near low vs BTC" ranking.

Two coins on different exchanges can share a ticker. The merge cannot tell them apart, so check the venue letters on a row if something looks off.

Fundamental layer (shown, not scored)

Dev activity, news velocity, catalysts, staking and unlocks are fetched for the top candidates and when you open a coin. They stay out of the composite because they cover only some coins and have no free history to backtest.

  • Dev surge: last 4 weeks of commits at least 1.5× the prior 12-week pace, with 20+ commits. Dormant: zero commits in 12 weeks.
  • News spike: 7-day article count at least 2× the prior 3-week weekly average.
  • Catalyst: mainnet, upgrade, listing, partnership, ETF, burn or airdrop in headlines within 21 days, or a GitHub release within 21 days.
  • Unlock risk: 1% or more of circulating supply unlocking within 30 days.

Risk flags (not scored)

  • Pump-dump history: a +30% day in the last 180 that fell back to within 5% of its pre-pump close inside 14 days.
  • Thin book: ±2% depth across all venues under $20k, or more than 1% slippage on a $10k buy at the best venue.
  • Dilution: circulating supply under 50% of total.

How validation works

The price-layer composite is recomputed at up to 18 past dates, 14 days apart, using only data up to each date (about 8 months). Its rank correlation (IC) with the next 14 calendar days of return is measured at each date.

Rule v3, fixed 31 Oct 2026 before any v3 result was seen: pass only if mean IC ≥ 0.05, permutation p < 0.05 (2000 shuffles) and a one-sided t-test of the per-date ICs gives p < 0.05.

Why the t-test was added: shuffling scores treats every coin as independent. When a market phase favours one type of coin for a few weeks, the shuffle test fires on noise. In a simulation with that kind of shared factor, the v2 rule passed 23–25% of pure-noise runs; v3 passed 3%.

Forward test: the first scan of each UTC day saves its scores in this browser and checks them 14 days later. That is the only test free of hindsight.

Known weaknesses: only coins listed today (survivorship), at most 18 observations, all in one stretch of market history.

Reading the scope

Candles: one amber phosphor. A lit, glowing capsule closed up; an unlit, hollow one closed down. Below the price runs a mirrored volume waveform, brightest in the 3-day anomaly window. Pink sparks mark pump-dump days.

vs BTC: the top panel overlays the USDT price (amber) and the coin/BTC ratio (cyan), each on its own scale. The bottom panel is the rolling 30-day correlation of daily returns with BTC, drawn as a seismogram: cyan above zero, pink below.
